Kulprit

What you ate. What followed.

A food and symptom log that counts, and leaves the conclusions to you and your clinician.

No account. No servers. Your data, your phone, your backup.

What it does

  • Log a meal in a few taps, or just stamp the time and fill it in later.
  • Log a reaction, with symptoms and how strong it was.
  • Log the things that are not food — a detergent, a pet, a sting.
  • See how often you had something, and how often something followed — and how often nothing did. Both halves, always.
  • Export the whole record as a file to take to an appointment.

What it will not do

Kulprit counts. It does not interpret, it does not single out one food over the others, and it never tells you a food is safe or unsafe.

Counts from your own log are not a finding. Two things happening near each other is a reason to ask someone qualified, not an answer.

Where your information lives

On your phone, in the app's own storage. There is no account to create and no server to send it to. If you back your phone up, that backup contains a copy — yours, held under Apple's or Google's terms — and it is what brings your log back on a new phone. The privacy page says all of this in full.
