Privacy

Last updated 20 September 2026

No account. No servers. Your data, your phone, your backup.

There is nothing to sign up for, and the app does not send what you log to us or to anyone else.

This page explains where your information actually lives, and — just as importantly — the one place a copy of it can end up that is not the app.

What Kulprit stores

Everything you enter — meals, ingredients, dishes, reactions and symptoms, non-food exposures, medications, times and dates, and the settings you choose — is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your phone.

We do not receive any of it. There is no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code in the app that reports your use of it back to anyone.

Backups — the honest part

If you back up your phone, the backup includes Kulprit's data. On iPhone that means iCloud Backup; on Android it means Android's own backup to your Google account, where you have it switched on.

That copy is yours, held under Apple's or Google's terms rather than ours, and it is what lets your log come back when you set up a new phone. We cannot see it and we cannot reach it.

If you would rather it did not happen, both platforms let you turn backup off for individual apps in your phone's settings.

We say this plainly because a claim that data never leaves the device would not be true, and an app about keeping an honest record should not begin with a claim it cannot keep.

Files you create yourself

Kulprit can write two kinds of file when you ask it to: a backup file you can restore from, and an export of your log for your own use or to show a clinician. Both are created only when you tap the control that creates them, and they are saved wherever you choose to put them. Once a file is in a folder you picked, it is governed by that folder — if that is a cloud drive, the file is in the cloud drive.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled by your phone and delivered by your phone. No reminder is sent from a server, and nothing about it leaves the device.

Supporting Kulprit

If you choose to make a contribution inside the app, the purchase is handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name, or your address — Apple tells us only that a purchase happened. Nothing you have logged is involved in a purchase in any way.

Health information, and consent

What you record in Kulprit is personal and can be sensitive. The app asks for your consent before anything about your health is recorded, and nothing about your health is written before you give it. You can read what you agreed to, and withdraw it, from You → Your data → What I agreed to.

Removing your data

You → Your data → Erase removes what you have logged, either a date range or all of it. It happens on the device and it is final — we hold no copy to delete on our side, because we never had one. Deleting the app also removes its storage; a phone backup made earlier may still contain a copy until that backup is replaced or removed.

Children

Kulprit is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone. Since the app collects nothing at all, this is a statement about intent rather than about data we hold.

Changes to this page

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top will say when. If a change ever meant that information started leaving your device, it would be said here plainly and in the app before it happened.
