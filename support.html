Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and nothing to log in to. The app works the moment you open it.

Where is my information kept?

On your phone. If you back your phone up, that backup includes a copy. The privacy page explains it properly.

I am getting a new phone. How do I bring my log across?

Two ways, and the first is simpler:

  • Restore the new phone from your phone backup — Kulprit's data comes with it.
  • Or, before you switch: You → Your data → Backup file, save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, then on the new phone use Restore from backup.

Restoring replaces what is on the phone rather than merging with it, so restore onto a fresh install rather than on top of a log you want to keep.

Can I get my log out as a document?

Yes. You → Your data → Export everything writes the whole record as a file you can keep or take to an appointment.

How do I delete everything?

You → Your data → Erase. A date range or all of it. It happens on your phone and it cannot be undone. Deleting the app removes its storage too.

Why will it not tell me which food is the problem?

Because counting cannot tell you that, and an app that pretended otherwise would be worse than useless — you might avoid the wrong food for months. Kulprit shows you how often you had something, and how often something followed, including how often nothing followed. What that means is a conversation with a clinician.

Why does it say “needs 4”?

Because below four occurrences a number says almost nothing. Until something has come up at least four times, the app tells you how many more it needs instead of showing you a figure you would over-trust.

Is it free?

Yes, and everything in it is free for everyone. There is an optional way to support the work inside the app; it unlocks nothing but a different app icon, and every amount unlocks the same one.

I supported the app and got a new phone.

Open Support Kulprit and use Restore Purchases.

Something is wrong with the app

Email me with what happened. If it is something about a number on screen looking wrong, a screenshot helps enormously, because nothing is reported from your phone and I cannot see what you are seeing.

If you are unwell, this is not the place. Kulprit is a log, not advice. If a reaction is severe or getting worse, contact a medical professional or your local emergency number.
